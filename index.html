Do I have to book a call to get anything out of this?

No. If you're not ready to pay for a call, send me your problem in a few sentences and I'll send back three things to consider within 2 business days, free. No scheduling, no call, just a real answer.

What does working with you actually look like?

It starts with a call. You tell me what's broken, I ask questions until we find the real problem underneath it, and we leave with next steps. Sometimes that's the whole thing. Sometimes it turns into scoped work or something ongoing. Depends on what the problem needs.

Do you just tell me what's wrong, or do you actually fix it?

Both. Some problems just need a second brain to think them through. Some need someone to actually build the fix, an automation, a flow, a piece of tech. I do whichever the problem calls for.

What's off the table?

Anything that needs a big team or a long build cycle. I work in small, focused pieces. If your problem needs six months and five people, I'm not your best fit, but I can probably tell you that in the first call.

What kind of problems do you actually work on?

Strategy, tech, and automation, and usually all three tangled together. Retention problems, manual processes that shouldn't be manual anymore, decisions you're making alone that you shouldn't have to.

How much does it cost?

The Call is $40/hour, no commitment. Scoped and Embedded work depend on what you need. We'll figure out the number together once we know the shape of the problem.

Why should I trust you with this?

I've built two real businesses from the ground up, FABLE+42 and Alongside, both still running. I'm not consulting from theory. Whatever's broken in your business, I've probably broken it myself first.
